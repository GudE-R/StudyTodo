## English (英語)

### Google Play Store

#### App Name (30 chars max)
StudyTodo - Focus Timer & ToDo

#### Short Description (80 chars max)
Ultimate ToDo for learners. Spaced Repetition & Pomodoro timer.

#### Full Description (4000 chars max)
**StudyTodo** is an all-in-one ToDo app designed to maximize learning efficiency.
No more wondering "what" or "when" to study.

**Key Features:**

🍅 **Pomodoro Timer**
Built-in optimal timer for maintaining focus. Repeat 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of break to digest tasks efficiently.

🧠 **Spaced Repetition System (SRS)**
Never forget what you've learned. Automatically generates review tasks at optimal timing based on the scientifically proven "Forgetting Curve". Perfect for memorizing vocabulary or studying for certification exams.

📅 **Routine Management**
Routine function to establish daily study habits. Automatically creates today's task list according to the schedule you set.

🗓️ **Intuitive Schedule Integration**
Seamless integration of calendar and schedule. Just long-press on a free time slot to instantly add a task for that date and time. Skip the tedious input and plan your study the moment you think of it.

📊 **Visualize Your Learning**
Check your efforts at a glance with heatmaps and statistical graphs. Analyze changes in study time and concentration to maintain motivation.

🎨 **Beautiful Design**
Dark mode supported. Create your own study space with color settings for each category and icon customization.

**Recommended for:**
- Students and working professionals aiming for exams or certifications
- Those who want to efficiently master memorization subjects
- Those who want to increase concentration with the Pomodoro Technique
- Those who are not good at making study plans

StudyTodo takes your learning to the next level.
